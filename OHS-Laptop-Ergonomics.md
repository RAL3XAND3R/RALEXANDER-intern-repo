# OHS Laptop Ergonomics Reflection

## My Current Workspace

My current workspace is pretty simple. I use a normal desk that I actually find quite good for working, together with my laptop and an external monitor.

The main thing I noticed is that my chair is probably the weakest part of my setup. It is a regular kitchen chair, so it works, but after sitting for a long time it can become uncomfortable.

I also tend to use my laptop in a way that is not ideal for long work sessions, especially because the screen position can make me look down more than I should.

## Risks of Working on a Laptop

One of the main problems with laptops is that the screen and keyboard are connected. This makes it difficult to have both of them in the best position at the same time.

If I position the laptop so that the keyboard is comfortable, the screen is usually too low. This can make me bend my neck forward for long periods of time.

Doing this repeatedly can contribute to neck, shoulder, back, and wrist discomfort. It can also make me feel more tired after working for several hours.

I think this is easy to ignore because when I am focused on coding, I don't always notice my posture until I have already been sitting for a long time.

## Equipment That Could Improve My Setup

Since I already have an external monitor, I don't think I need to completely change my workspace.

The main improvement I would make is using the monitor as my main screen and keeping the laptop in a secondary position.

An external keyboard and mouse would also help because they would let me position the laptop or monitor at a more comfortable height without having to worry about where the keyboard is.

Another improvement I would consider is getting a proper office chair. My current chair is usable, but it is not particularly comfortable for long work sessions.

I don't think I need an expensive chair just for the sake of having an ergonomic setup. I would mainly look for something that gives me good back support and allows me to adjust the height properly.

## How I Can Improve My Setup

For longer work sessions, I would like to use the external monitor as my main display and position it so that I can look at it without constantly bending my neck.

I can also keep the keyboard and mouse at a comfortable height and distance from me.

The desk itself is not something I feel I need to replace. It is a normal desk, but it gives me enough space to arrange my equipment comfortably.

The chair is the part I would eventually like to improve the most.

## Behavioural Changes

Changing the equipment is only part of the solution.

One thing I need to work on is remembering to move during the day. When I am coding or focused on a task, it is easy to lose track of time and stay in the same position for much longer than I intended.

I can take short breaks to stand up, walk around, stretch a little, or simply get away from the screen for a few minutes.

I can also pay more attention to my posture while I am working. I don't expect to maintain one perfect posture all day, but I can notice when I start leaning forward or sitting in an uncomfortable position and adjust myself.

## Using Focus Bear for Breaks

One thing that could help me with this is using Focus Bear to remind me to take movement breaks.

The problem is not necessarily that I don't know that I should take breaks. The problem is that when I am focused on something, I can forget about them completely.

Having a reminder means I don't have to rely entirely on remembering to stop working.

I could use these reminders during longer work sessions to stand up, move around, and give my eyes a break.

## What I Noticed

After thinking about my workspace more carefully, I realized that my setup is not actually that bad.

I already have an external monitor and a decent-sized desk, so I don't need to completely redesign everything.

The main areas I could improve are the chair and the way I position my laptop and monitor.

I also noticed that the biggest problem can sometimes be my habits rather than the equipment. Even with a good setup, staying in the same position for several hours is still not ideal.

## One Practical Change I Made

The main change I want to make is to rely more on my external monitor when I am working for long periods and position it at a comfortable height.

I also want to start using movement reminders so that I don't accidentally spend several hours sitting without getting up.

In the future, I would also like to replace my current kitchen chair with a proper office chair because I think that would make the biggest difference to my comfort.

## Final Reflection

The main thing I learned from this topic is that ergonomics is not necessarily about buying expensive equipment.

A lot of it comes down to how the equipment is positioned and how I behave while working.

In my case, I already have some useful equipment, especially my external monitor and desk. The main things I can improve are my chair, my screen position, and remembering to take movement breaks.

As a developer, I spend a lot of time sitting in front of a computer, so I think these small changes are worth making. I would rather make the workspace comfortable now than wait until I start having constant pain or discomfort.

For me, the biggest takeaway is that a good workspace should make it easy to work comfortably without having to constantly think about it.
