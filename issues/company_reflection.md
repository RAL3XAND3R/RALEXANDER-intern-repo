# Company Reflection

## Why do you think Focus Bear was created?

I think Focus Bear was created because the company realized that many people have trouble concentrating and staying focused. There are already ways to block distracting websites and applications, but Focus Bear seems to offer a more complete alternative for people who need help staying focused.

## What problem is Focus Bear solving?

Focus Bear is trying to solve the problem of people spending hours on distracting or unproductive applications such as TikTok and YouTube.

It is easy to open one of these apps for a few minutes and then realize that a lot of time has passed. This can make people lose focus on the things they actually need to do, such as studying, working, or completing important tasks.

## Why do you think this mission is important?

I think this is important because distractions can have a big impact on how people spend their time. Someone can have something important to do but keep getting distracted until several hours have passed.

Having a tool that helps reduce those distractions can make it easier for people to focus on what they actually need to accomplish.

## How does Focus Bear’s work align with your personal values or interests?

I don't think I have a strong personal connection to the mission from a values perspective, but I am interested in the problem because I can relate to having trouble staying focused.

As someone who studies and works with software development, being able to stay focused is important. I think tools that can reduce unnecessary distractions can be useful in that kind of environment.

## Do you personally relate to any of the challenges that Focus Bear aims to solve?

Yes. I have been diagnosed with ADHD, so I can personally relate to some of the challenges that Focus Bear is trying to solve.

One of the things I experience is losing track of what I am supposed to be doing. I can start with one task and quickly get distracted or lose my direction.

Because of this, I understand why having something that helps reduce distractions and keeps me focused could be useful.

## How might Focus Bear help someone with similar challenges?

I think Focus Bear could help someone by reducing the things that constantly distract them and making it easier to stay focused on a specific task.

For someone who has trouble maintaining focus, having fewer distractions around them could make it easier to stay on track with studying or working.

I don't think a tool can completely solve these problems by itself, but I think it can make the environment easier to work in and give the person some extra support when they are trying to focus.
