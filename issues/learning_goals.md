# Learning Goals

## What do you hope to learn from this internship?

I hope to learn what it is actually like to work in a real professional environment. I want to improve my backend development skills and become more comfortable using Git in a real development workflow.

I also want to understand how the things I have learned at university are applied when working on a real project.

## What skills are you most excited to develop?

I am most excited to improve my programming skills, especially in backend development.

Even though I am currently focused on backend development during this internship, I would like to become a full-stack developer in the future. I think getting a strong foundation in backend development will help me with that.

I am also interested in learning more about software architecture and how real applications are structured.

## What do you think you can contribute to the team?

I think I can contribute with the knowledge I have gained from university and my willingness to learn.

I have learned different programming and software development concepts at university, and I can use that knowledge while working on the project. I also want to contribute by working well with the team, asking questions when I need help, and trying to solve problems instead of giving up when something is difficult.

## What is one area you feel less confident in that you’d like to improve?

I don't feel completely confident in my programming skills yet, and I would like to improve them during this internship.

I also want to improve my English, especially when communicating in a professional environment. I think working with an international team will give me a good opportunity to practice and become more comfortable communicating in English.

## What does success in this internship look like for you?

For me, success would mean finishing the internship with a much better understanding of how a real software development environment works.

I want this internship to give me a good example of the professional world, including how teams communicate, how people work together, how tasks are organized, and how software is developed in a real environment.

I would also like to finish the internship feeling more confident in my programming and backend skills than I did when I started.
